Connecting

No passwords. No servers. Two minutes.

TikTok

Type your username and press Connect. LiveBridge reads the same public data a viewer of your live sees. No TikTok password is ever asked.

YouTube

Sign in with Google in your browser. Only one permission is requested: read-only access to your YouTube account, to read your own live chat and counters. You can disconnect, and revoke it at Google, from Settings.

Questions

FAQ

Which platforms are supported?

TikTok LIVE and YouTube Live. Twitch and Kick are planned but not available yet.

Which systems?

The installer is for Windows and OBS Studio. It finds your OBS folder and lets you confirm or change it before installing anything.

Windows says the app is unknown. Is it safe?

The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you. Choose "More info" then "Run anyway". The plugin only runs inside OBS and talks only to TikTok and Google.

Does it send my chat anywhere?

No. Everything stays on your computer. See the Privacy Policy.

Can it moderate my chat?

Not yet. It shows what happens (for example YouTube bans and timeouts) but it does not act on your behalf.

Why does TikTok show fewer details than the TikTok app?

TikTok does not offer an official live API. LiveBridge reads the public data of your live, so some app-only items (for example some emoji codes) can look different.
