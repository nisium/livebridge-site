Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

In short: LiveBridge runs only on your computer. We have no servers, we do not collect your data, and nothing you do in LiveBridge is sent to us or to anyone other than TikTok and Google, which you connect to yourself.

Who we are

LiveBridge ("the app") is an OBS Studio plugin made by NLG. Contact: GitHub Issues.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube (if you connect it). With your consent through Google sign-in, the app reads your own live broadcast chat messages, Super Chats, new members, moderation events and your channel's live and subscriber counts, through the YouTube Data API. The only permission requested is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly (read only).
  • TikTok (if you connect it). You type a TikTok username. The app reads the public data of that live (chat, gifts, follows, shares, counters), like a viewer would. No TikTok password is requested or stored.
  • Settings. Your choices (username, which counters to show, layout) are stored locally by OBS on your computer.

Where data goes

  • Chat messages, events and counters are shown in the app and kept in memory while it runs. They are not written to disk. The only other files are a cache of TikTok's public gift list (names and pictures of gifts, no personal data) and an optional debug log that is off by default and that you enable yourself.
  • A helper service started by the app listens only on 127.0.0.1 (your own computer). It is not reachable from the network.
  • The app contacts only TikTok and Google/YouTube servers, to get the data above. It has no analytics, no advertising and no telemetry.
  • We do not receive, store, share or sell any of your data, and no human at NLG can read it.

Google sign-in token

After you sign in with Google, the app keeps a refresh token in a file in your user profile (%APPDATA%\LiveBridge on Windows) so you do not have to sign in again. It never leaves your computer. When you press Disconnect the app revokes the permission at Google and deletes the file. You can also remove LiveBridge any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

LiveBridge's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to show your own live chat and counters to you inside OBS. It is not used for advertising, not transferred to third parties and not used to train any model.

Other people's messages

Chat messages come from your viewers. The app shows them to you and does not keep them after you close OBS.

Children

The app is a tool for streamers and is not directed to children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions or requests: GitHub Issues.
